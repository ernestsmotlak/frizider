# Task: create a recipe from a list of ingredients

The input is a list of ingredients the user has at home.

Create one realistic recipe that uses as many of the listed ingredients as possible. You may
assume basic pantry staples such as salt, pepper, water, and cooking oil are available; do
not build the recipe around anything else that is not listed.

Keep it practical for a home cook: a short name, a brief description, a realistic number of
servings, realistic preparation and cooking times in minutes, and short concrete steps in
the order they are performed.
